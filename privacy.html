Privacy

Houseline is an app for home, car and yard upkeep and family readiness. This page says plainly what it keeps and where.

If you never sign in

Everything stays on your own phone or browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Settings to download a backup now and then.

If you sign in

  • What is stored: your email address, your tasks, vehicles, appliances, supplies, history, emergency notes and any photos you attach. They are stored with Supabase, the hosting service the app uses, so your phones can stay in sync.
  • Family profiles: the Family screen can hold, for each person or pet in your home, a name, phone, allergies, blood type, health notes, doctor or vet and things worth knowing. This is optional. It is stored with the rest of your household data, so everyone who shares your household can see and edit it. It is included in the daily backups (kept 14 days, so a deleted entry can linger there up to 14 days) and in "Download all my data", which is not encrypted. Please do not enter Social Security, insurance or policy numbers. This information is never put in reminders or crash reports.
  • Private health notes: on a Family profile you can choose to keep allergies and health notes private. They are then stored in a separate place that only your own account can read. They are not part of the shared household data, the shared daily backups, other members' views or "Download all my data" from their accounts. Deleting your account deletes them.
  • View-only members: the owner can invite someone as view-only. They can see the shared household data but cannot change it.
  • Join notices: when someone joins your household, the app records their email address in a short-lived notice, used only to send the other members a "someone joined" notification. Notices are deleted after 7 days.
  • Who can see it: you, and anyone you invite to share your household. Nobody else can read it through the app.
  • Backups: a daily copy of your household data is kept for 14 days so a mistake can be undone. Photos are not part of these copies.
  • Reminders: if you turn on phone reminders, your phone's notification address is stored along with the time and quiet days you chose, only to send you those reminders. Turning reminders off or signing out removes it.
  • Crash reports: if the app hits an error it may send a short error message (no task contents) so the problem can be fixed. A limited number are kept.
  • Sign-in with Google: if you use it, Google tells the app your email address. Nothing else is requested.

What is not done

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your information, and no analytics.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More, then Account, and choose Delete my account. That removes your email, household data (if you are the only member) and photos. You can also download everything first with "Download all my data".

Problem reports

"Copy info for a problem report" in Settings only copies the app version, your browser name and screen size to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into a message yourself.
